## Remnant 2 Toolkit (build planner stats)

`src/Remnant2Overlay.Companion/wwwroot/data/planner.json` is generated by `tools/build_planner_data.py` from the item
lists in [Remnant 2 Toolkit](https://github.com/joshpayette/remnant2-toolkit) (commit
2cbcd28d2b7df429612be6f677e77b6d912253b8), Copyright Josh Payette and contributors, licensed under the
GNU General Public License v3.0 (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html). The data was converted to JSON and
trimmed to the fields the planner uses.

## Game assets

Remnant 2 names, data and icons belong to Gunfire Games. Icons are extracted locally from your own
game install into a local cache and are not committed to this repository.
